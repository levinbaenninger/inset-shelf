# Code of conduct

Everyone who participates in Inset Shelf issues, reviews, and other project spaces should be able to ask questions, disagree, and contribute safely. Explain objections to a change in terms another contributor can act on. Respect people's time, identities, and privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing someone's private information are grounds for moderation. Maintainers may remove content, close a discussion, or restrict participation in response.

Report a concern privately to the repository owner at [levin@baenninger.me](mailto:levin@baenninger.me). Include a link to the discussion if available. If the concern involves the owner, you can [report abuse directly to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
