## Does it run on Windows?

Yes, on Windows 11. The installer sits beside the Mac builds for Apple silicon and Intel. See [Install](/docs/install#windows).

## Why is the download blocked at first?

The builds are not signed with a developer certificate. On a Mac, click **Open Anyway** in **System Settings > Privacy & Security** once. On Windows, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## How does it update?

The Mac builds and the Windows installer check for a newer build at launch and every four hours. The portable Windows zip does not update itself. See [Updates and versions](/docs/updates).

## Where does the shelf keep my items?

On your computer, in one file called `shelf.json`, with images and files in a folder beside it. See [Where your data lives](/docs/data).

## Double Shift does nothing. Why?

On a Mac, check that **Inset Shelf** is allowed under **Accessibility** in **System Settings > Privacy & Security**. On macOS 27, the list is called **Device Control and Data Access**. The shelf shows a **Double Shift is off** banner with **Turn On…** when it needs access.

On Windows, some apps ignore the `Ctrl+Insert` the shelf sends to copy. If the shelf opens without saving your selection, check whether that app copies it with `Ctrl+Insert`.

## Is Inset Shelf finished?

No. It is a free test build that changes as people try it. If something breaks, [report it on GitHub](https://github.com/levinbaenninger/inset-shelf/issues).
