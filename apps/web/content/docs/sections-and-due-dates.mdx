## Sections

The list shows every section as a group. Click a section's name to fold it away.

- The filled dot at the right of a section's name shows where new items go. Click another section's dot to send new items there, or press `⌘1` to `⌘9`.
- Type `# Name` in the note field and press `Return` to make a section, or use **New Section** in the **⋯** at the top.
- A section's **⋯**, or a right-click on its name, renames it, clears its checked items or deletes it.
- Drag an item onto another section to move it there, or press `⌥⌘↑` and `⌥⌘↓`.

## Details

Every item can also hold a **Description**, **Next steps**, a **Due** date and [linked windows](/docs/linked-windows). Selecting an item shows them; `→`, or **Details**, opens the details view with everything about the item and an **Add** menu for the fields it doesn't have yet. Edits save as you type.

Up to three small badges on the item's source line show the Due chip, step progress, linked windows and pins.

## Next steps

`⇧⌘S` adds a checklist under the item. In a step, `Return` adds the next one and `⌫` in an empty one removes it. Its progress shows on the source line, such as **1/3**.

## Due dates

`⇧⌘D` opens the Due picker: **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **This weekend**, **Next week**, **Pick a date…** and **Clear**.

- This weekend is the coming Saturday, and next week the coming Monday.
- The chip says Today, Tomorrow, a weekday within the week, or the date. It is blue when the item is due today and says **Overdue** in red after that.
- In the calendar, the arrow keys move the day, `Return` picks it and `Esc` closes it.

Search filters by due date: `due:today`, `due:overdue`, `due:week`, `due:any` and `due:none`. The filter button at the end of the search field picks them for you.
