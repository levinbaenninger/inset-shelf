---
title: Attachments
description: Add screenshots, images, files, and drawings to an item you already have.
---

Each item can hold up to 12 attachments besides its own text, image, or file.

## Add attachments

- Select an item, then drop files on its open row. The row highlights to show where they'll go. The notice tells you what was saved and offers **Undo** for the files that were added. A full item refuses the drop.
- Open **Details** and choose **Attach Files…**, **Paste Image**, or **New Drawing…** from **Add**. You can also press `⌘V` (`Ctrl+V` on Windows) in Details to attach a file or image from the clipboard.
- On a screenshot card, click **Add to “…”** when the card names the item you want. See [Screenshots](/docs/capture#screenshots).

## Open or remove an attachment

The row shows the first three attachments. Click the **+N** tile, if present, to see the rest in **Details**. Point at a tile to see its full name. In Details, moving the keyboard onto a tile shows its name too.

In Details, click a picture to draw on it. To edit its pin labels, `Tab` to its tile and use the fields under the attachments. **Move pins** opens the drawing window with the Pin tool. You can drag any attachment into another app as a file.

To remove an attachment, click the **×** on its tile in Details, or focus the tile there and press `⌫`. **Undo** or `⌘Z` puts the attachment back.
