---
title: Settings
description: Open at Login, where the icon shows, screenshot cards, recall, copying, and tips.
---

**Settings…** (`⌘,`, or **⋯** at the top of the shelf) holds:

- **Open at Login**.
- Where the app's icon shows: the Dock, the menu bar or both (macOS only).
- **Copying > Mark as done when copied** controls whether **Copy** and **Copy as List** check items off.
- **Screenshots > Show new screenshots** controls whether screenshot cards appear.
- **Recall > Show items from the window in front** controls the recall pill. It is on by default. See [Recall](/docs/sources#recall).
- **Tips** turns the one-time tips off. **Reset Tutorial** starts the checklist and tips over.

On Windows there is no menu bar. **Settings…** is in the tray icon's menu, in the shelf's top **⋯** menu and on `Ctrl+,`. That **⋯** menu also holds **Float on Top**, **Getting Started**, **Show Me Around** and **Help**, which opens these docs.
