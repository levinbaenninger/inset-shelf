---
title: Where your data lives
description: Everything stays on your computer, in one folder.
---

The shelf is stored in one file, `shelf.json`, with images and files in an `attachments` folder and the settings in `settings.json` next to it:

- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/Inset Shelf`
- Windows: `%APPDATA%\Inset Shelf`

The folder can hold two kinds of recovery copy beside `shelf.json`:

- `shelf.json.prev` is the shelf as it was before the last save. If `shelf.json` is missing at launch, the app tries this copy. The app keeps the images and files listed by the recovery copy when it tidies attachments at launch.
- `shelf.unreadable-<date>.json` is a damaged shelf the app set aside at launch. The app moves it aside under that name, starts a new shelf, and keeps the images and files the old one names.

If the app can't open the shelf file at all, or can't move a damaged one aside, it leaves the file where it is, makes no dated copy, and doesn't save changes to the shelf for that session. Files you drop and images you paste can still be copied into `attachments`, but the shelf won't list them after a restart. The shelf shows a notice that says why.

Quit the app and delete the folder to remove the shelf and every copy of it.

The app has no account and sends your items nowhere. It goes online to check for [updates](/docs/updates); see [Privacy](/privacy) for the details.

## Saving

Edits save as you type. The app writes a temporary file first, then renames it into place. If the shelf can't be saved, a **Not saved to disk** notice explains why. An affected item shows **Not saved** with **Retry** and **Discard changes**. Discarding restores the item's last saved version. An item that has never been saved offers **Delete** instead.

When more than one banner appears, the most important one stays open. Click **2 more**, or the count shown, to see the others. Use **Show full message** to read a long notice and **Show fewer** to collapse the extra banners again.

When you quit or restart to update, the app waits for shelf edits and unsaved drawing marks. If it can't save them, choose **Retry** to save and quit, **Quit without saving** to leave, or **Cancel** to keep the app open.

## Deleting and undo

Deleting an item shows a notice with **Undo**. `⌘Z` brings back the last deleted item. You can undo up to ten deletions.
