Each kind of capture takes one step, and none of it asks you to fill anything in. New items go to the current section.

## Selected text, with Shift twice

Select text in any app and tap **Shift** twice. The shelf saves it with the app, window and page it came from, and the links inside it. A short notice beside the pointer confirms it when the shelf is hidden, and your clipboard is put back as it was.

With nothing selected, Shift twice shows or hides the shelf.

A few things to know:

- The app copies your selection by sending `⌘C` (`Ctrl+Insert` on Windows) to the app in front. Clipboard history tools may still record that copy.
- An app that ignores `Ctrl+Insert` copies nothing, so there double Shift only shows or hides the shelf.
- In VS Code, Cursor and some other editors, copying with nothing selected copies the whole line, so double Shift saves that line.
- Double Shift inside the shelf only hides it, so the shelf never saves its own text.

## Screenshots

Take a screenshot as usual, and a card with it appears beside the shelf. Hover it to **Copy** it or **Add to Shelf**. Its corners dismiss it, keep it on screen, annotate it or save it to Downloads, and you can drag it into another app. Unless kept, it goes away on its own after a few seconds.

- On macOS that is a screenshot to the clipboard or to a file.
- On Windows it is one that Snipping Tool (`Win+Shift+S` or `PrtScn`) or `Win+PrtScn` saves to the Screenshots folder. A screenshot that only goes to the clipboard (`Alt+PrtScn`) shows no card.

**Settings** turns the cards off.

## Drawings and numbered pins

**File > New Drawing…** (`⌥⌘N`, or **+** in the note field) opens a drawing window with pen, line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse and text tools. The tick at the end of its toolbar (**Save**, `⌘↩`) adds the drawing to the shelf, and the copy button before it also puts it on the clipboard.

Double-click an image on the shelf to draw on it, then **Save** to replace it. The **Pin** tool (`P`) places numbered map pins:

- Click to drop pin 1, 2, 3…, and drag one to move it.
- `1`–`9` select a pin, the arrow keys nudge it (`⇧` for 10 px), and `⌫` removes it; the rest renumber.
- `⇧P` drops one at the pointer.

Pins stay editable beside the image. Their labels are set in the item's details.

## Files and pasted images

Drop files on the shelf, paste an image, or attach files with the **+** in the note field. Drag an image or file out of the shelf to drop it into another app. Images and files take a note under them.

## Notes and prompts

Type into the note field at the bottom of the shelf and press `Return`. Notes are Markdown, formatted as you type: `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `` `code` ``, lists, quotes and links. `⇧↩` starts a new line.
