---
title: Sources and reopening
description: The line under each item says where it came from, and opens it again.
---

The line under an item names the app, window and page it came from. Click it, or press `⌘O`, to open the page or the app again.

## How items are named

- A GitHub pull request or issue, a ChatGPT or Claude chat, a post on X or a YouTube video is named on the line.
- Other pages show their site and title.
- The page address comes from your browser: Safari, Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Firefox, Zen and other Chromium browsers.

## What opens

- A ChatGPT thread, a Claude chat or project and a Notion page open right where they were, in their desktop app.
- Figma, Slack and Linear come to the front.
- Other items open the page they came from.

On Windows, the page of a selection copied from Chrome, Edge, Firefox or an app built on Chromium is the one the browser noted with the copy. A desktop app opens only at a chat or page it has a link for, such as a Claude chat or a Notion page; other items open their page.

## When the line is missing parts

An item records the app, window title and page address only when the matching permission is granted. Without Screen Recording there is no title, and without Accessibility and Automation there is no address. See [Install](/docs/install#permissions).
