---
title: Sources and reopening
description: Open an item's source and find items from the window you're using.
---

The line under an item shows its source when the shelf could read it. Click the line to open the page or app again when there's a destination. On a text item, you can also press `⌘O`. On an image, `⌘O` opens the drawing tools.

## How items are named

- A GitHub pull request or issue, a ChatGPT or Claude chat, a post on X or a YouTube video is named on the line.
- Other pages show their site and title.
- On macOS, the shelf can read a page address from Safari, Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Firefox, Zen, and other Chromium browsers.

## What opens

- A saved app link can reopen a ChatGPT or T3 Code thread, a Claude chat or project, or a Notion page in its desktop app.
- On macOS, supported desktop apps come to the front if the shelf has no app link. This includes Figma, Slack, and Linear.
- If the shelf has a page address for another source, it opens that page.

On Windows, a selection can get its page address from the copy made by Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or an app built on Chromium. A desktop app needs a link to the chat, thread, or page to reopen it.

## When the line is missing parts

On macOS, Screen Recording lets the shelf read window titles. Accessibility lets it read page addresses. Safari and Chromium browsers can also ask for Automation access. Firefox and Zen use Accessibility to supply the address. See [Install](/docs/install#permissions).

## Recall

When you return to a window, page, or repository with open items, a small **3 items from here** pill can appear in the window's bottom-right corner. It counts unchecked items captured there or linked to that window. Click it to open the shelf filtered to those items. The pill leaves the keyboard with your app and goes away when you type or after a few seconds.

Recall can match a browser page by its address, a desktop thread by its link, or files in the same VS Code or Cursor project. It can also match the app and window title. On Windows, browser windows match by title when an address isn't available.

When you open the shelf beside a matching window, click the **from here** chip in Search. You can also choose **From this window** from the filter menu or type `from:here`. Click the pressed chip again or remove `from:here` to clear the filter.

While the filter is on, a note you type goes with that window, so it stays in the list and shows up there next time. A chip under the note field names the window. Click its **×** to type plain notes until you open the shelf or the filter again.

The pill shows at most once per visit. After the first visit, it waits until you've been away for at least ten minutes. The wait gets longer when you leave a pill alone. Click its **×** to quiet that place until its matching items change.

The pill is on by default. To turn it off, open **Settings > Recall** and switch off **Show items from the window in front**. The search filter stays available. On macOS, Recall uses the [permissions you grant for capture](/docs/install#permissions).
