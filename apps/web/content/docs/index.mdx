Inset Shelf docks next to the window you are working in and keeps what you want to come back to. Select text in another app and tap Shift twice to save it with any source details the shelf can read.

You can also keep:

- screenshots from the card that appears when you take them
- sketches and images with numbered pins
- files you drop or paste
- notes and prompts you type into the shelf

Check off items, copy them into another app, or drag them out. An item can also hold a description, a checklist, a due date, linked windows, and attachments.

Inset Shelf runs on macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and Windows 11. The app is free to download and use.

## Where to start

- [Install](/docs/install) the app and grant its permissions.
- Learn the ways to [capture](/docs/capture).
- Keep the [keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts) at hand.

## Using the shelf window

The shelf opens on the right of the window in front. If the right side is too narrow, it tries the left, then the inside of the right edge. With no window to dock to, it opens at the right of the screen. Press `Esc` or click the **×** at the top to hide it.

When you open the shelf, the note field is ready for typing unless you're already using an editor, Details, a menu, or a search with text in it.

## Getting started in the app

The **Getting started** checklist at the top of the shelf ticks off each task as you do it. Click a task for instructions, or use its **×** to close the checklist. Choose **Getting Started** from the menu bar icon on macOS or the tray icon on Windows to bring it back.

Choose **Show Me Around** from the app or tray menu to walk through the shelf one control at a time. Use the arrow keys or `Return` to move through the tour and `Esc` to close it.

The first run adds four example items. Use **Remove Examples** in the checklist's **⋯** menu to delete them.
