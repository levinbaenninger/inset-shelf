Inset Shelf docks next to the window you are working in and keeps what you want to come back to. Select text in any app and tap Shift twice, and it lands on the shelf with the app, window and web page it came from.

You can also keep:

- screenshots, from a card that appears as you take them;
- sketches and images with numbered pins;
- files you drop or paste;
- notes and prompts you type into the shelf.

Each item can be checked off, copied back into another app, or dragged out. Any item can hold a description, a checklist, a due date and linked windows, but none of it is asked for when you capture.

Inset Shelf runs on macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and Windows 11. The app is free to download and use.

## Where to start

- [Install](/docs/install) the app and grant its permissions.
- Learn the ways to [capture](/docs/capture).
- Keep the [keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts) at hand.

The app also teaches itself: a **Getting started** checklist at the top of the shelf ticks off each task as you really do it, and **Help > Show Me Around** walks through the shelf one control at a time.
