---
title: Copy with Details
description: Copy an item back out, with its link, steps, due date and windows.
---

Copying an item puts it back as it was captured: a saved selection pastes with its links and formatting, an image with its pins drawn in and its note, and a file as the file itself.

| Command | Keys | What it copies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Copy** | `⌘C` | The item as captured. |
| **Copy with Link** |  | The item and the address it came from. |
| **Copy with Details** | `⌥⌘C` | The item as Markdown, with its description, its steps as a `- [ ]` checklist, `Due:` and its linked windows. |
| **Copy as List** | `⇧⌘C` | Several picked items, or the open items of the current section, as a numbered list with their links. |

An item without details copies exactly as before. **Settings** can mark items as done when they are copied.

`⌘`-click picks several items and `⇧`-click a range; `⌘A` picks every item shown. With several picked, `⌘C` copies them as a list. Apps that paste formatting, such as Teams or Outlook, show the first image inside the list.
