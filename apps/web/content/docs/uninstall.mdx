---
title: Uninstall
description: Remove the app, and its data if you want to.
---

## macOS

1. Quit Inset Shelf from its menu bar icon or the **Inset Shelf** menu.
2. Drag **Inset Shelf** from **Applications** to the Trash.
3. To delete your items and settings too, delete `~/Library/Application Support/Inset Shelf`.

You can also remove **Inset Shelf** from the lists under **System Settings > Privacy & Security**.

## Windows

1. Right-click the Shelf mark in the notification area and quit the app.
2. Uninstall **Inset Shelf** from **Settings > Apps > Installed apps**. A portable zip copy only needs its folder deleted.
3. To delete your items and settings too, delete `%APPDATA%\Inset Shelf`.
